# Product

## Register

product

## Users

A two-person household managing prepared meals in a freezer. They use MealDeck while stocking meals, checking nutrition, and making a quick dinner decision without repeating recent meals.

## Product Purpose

MealDeck keeps freezer inventory accurate and makes dinner selection effortless. Success means users can trust the box count, see carbs clearly, and accept or repeat a random draw without uncertainty about what changed.

## Brand Personality

Practical, calm, and friendly. The voice should be direct and reassuring, with enough warmth to make a routine household task pleasant.

## Anti-references

Avoid gamified food-app styling, decorative restaurant imagery, novelty interactions, and ambiguous actions that hide inventory changes.

## Design Principles

- Make inventory consequences explicit before repeated or destructive actions.
- Keep the dinner decision fast while preserving user control.
- Put carb and serving information where decisions happen.
- Treat the cooking code as appliance-operating information and keep it prominent.
- Keep card identifiers distinct until the user has reviewed them.
- Exclude shipping details when a cropped packing-slip item table is sufficient.
- Prefer familiar controls and plain language over novelty.
- Keep local and server experiences behaviorally consistent.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA. Use clear accessible labels, adequate color contrast, touch targets of at least 44 by 44 points, dynamic text-safe layouts, and reduced-motion alternatives where motion is introduced.
